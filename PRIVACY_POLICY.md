# Uni Leca Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 10 September 2026**

Uni Leca is an offline university attendance and timetable application. This privacy policy explains how the Android application handles information.

## Information stored by the app

Uni Leca stores the information you enter, including semester names, module names, timetable sessions, attendance records, attendance thresholds and app preferences. This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your Android device.

## Data collection and transmission

The production-v2 build does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, social-login, Firebase AI, or other network data-collection SDKs. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Uni Leca does not transmit your attendance data to the developer or to a Uni Leca server.

## Notifications

If you choose to enable attendance reminders, Uni Leca requests Android notification permission on supported Android versions. Timetable information required to schedule those reminders remains on the device. You can disable reminders at any time in the app or in Android system settings.

## Device restart permission

Uni Leca uses Android's RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED permission only to restore locally scheduled attendance reminders after a device restart.

## Backups and exports

Android automatic cloud backup is disabled for Uni Leca. The app provides manual backup and report export features. A backup or report is written only to a location you choose through Android's system file picker. Once exported, the file is outside Uni Leca's private app storage and its protection depends on the location and services you choose to store or share it with.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual attendance marks, modules, timetable sessions, or entire semesters from within the app. Deleting a semester permanently deletes the modules, sessions, and attendance records belonging to that semester. Uninstalling Uni Leca removes its private local application data according to Android behavior, unless you separately created exported backup/report files.

## Children

Uni Leca is a general education productivity tool and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information on a developer-controlled server because it does not operate such a data-collection service.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, accounts, advertising, or another feature that changes data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be updated before that version is released.
