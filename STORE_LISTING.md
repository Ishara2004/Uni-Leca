# Uni Leca — Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App name
Uni Leca

## Short description
Track university attendance, timetable sessions and module attendance risk offline.

## Full description
Uni Leca is an offline attendance and timetable companion built for university students who need a simple way to keep track of classes and attendance requirements.

Create semesters and modules, build your weekly timetable, record Present, Absent, Medical or No Class status, and see how your attendance compares with each module's required threshold.

### Key features

- Track attendance separately for Lectures, Tutorials and Labs within each module.
- See overall module attendance and session-type percentages at a glance.
- View risk insights, including what your percentage would become after another missed session and how many attended sessions may be needed to recover.
- Add one-off extra lectures, labs or tutorials to a specific date without changing the weekly timetable.
- Jump directly to any date with the calendar picker.
- Receive optional after-class attendance reminders and record attendance from notification actions.
- Keep multiple semesters and review semester history.
- Export attendance reports as PDF or CSV.
- Create and restore versioned local backups.
- Use a home-screen widget for today's current or next session.
- Works without an account and without an internet connection.

### Privacy-first by design

Uni Leca stores academic tracking data locally on your device. The production build does not include advertising, analytics, cloud-sync or AI/network SDKs and does not require an account.

### Important note

Uni Leca is a personal tracking tool. Attendance calculations are based on the information you enter and may not match every university's official rules. Always verify eligibility and medical-attendance rules with your institution.
